# Lights Out v11.3.4

A look-and-feel release. Nothing about timers, power actions or lights
changes.

**The icon is back.** The app icon is the night badge with the logo's broken
"O" lit in teal, the same O as the wordmark and the Tonight dial. Large sizes
show the dark lamp leaning in with its beam; taskbar sizes are the bold O,
drawn to stay sharp at every scale.

**The wordmark leads.** On windows 720 pixels tall or more, the Lights Out
wordmark is larger and its lamp beam brighter, reaching across to the dial.

**A night room.** The background is a deep night-blue space (lamp light at
the top left, a cool moonlit wash, a soft glow under the dial) that still
darkens as a run counts down.

Assets: `Lights-Out-Setup-v11.3.4.exe`, `Lights-Out-Portable-v11.3.4-win-x64.zip`,
`SHA256SUMS.txt`, `BUILD-MANIFEST.json` and `WINDOWS-VERIFICATION.json`. Both
packages are unsigned.
