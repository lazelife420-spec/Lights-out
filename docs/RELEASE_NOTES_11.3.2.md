# Lights Out v11.3.2

A design release. Nothing about timers, power actions or lights changes.

**Tonight.** The dial leads with the time: a small "Local time" label above
it and your session name below (click it to rename). While a run is going the
dial shows only the countdown and the clock, and the plan beside it names the
end. The plan card's headline is when tonight ends; Duration and Ending line
up; Cancel lines up with Pause and Snooze. Test Mode is shown by the pill,
the Safety strip and the footer, and a real action still shows its red chip
on the dial.

**Schedule.** Bedtime rules and One night only come straight after Next
bedtime, with the month planner, Calendars and import/export below. With
nothing scheduled, the top card says so in words, and calendar days are
compact.

**Devices.** Each card shows its state at the top right of its header.

**Night Modes and Proof.** Modes that are not available yet read as a quiet
footnote. Before your first run, Proof says "No runs yet" once.

Assets: `Lights-Out-Setup-v11.3.2.exe`, `Lights-Out-Portable-v11.3.2-win-x64.zip`,
`SHA256SUMS.txt`, `BUILD-MANIFEST.json` and `WINDOWS-VERIFICATION.json`. Both
packages are unsigned.
